Farm credit passport

Next

Each season's plan, inputs and harvest become a verified record a lender can read. That gives farmers without formal credit history a path to fair input loans instead of informal moneylenders.

Claim-ready insurance evidence

Next

Geo-tagged, dated crop photos and a local weather log are gathered as the season happens. When a crop fails, the farmer already has the evidence a crop-insurance claim asks for.

WhatsApp and missed call

Next

Farmers get the same advice over WhatsApp and a voice line, so it reaches those with basic phones and patchy data.

Pooled selling

Next

Neighbouring farms combine loads to share one truck and sell together, so a small harvest gets the rate of a large one.

Water and carbon credits

Next

Practices the app already tracks, like drip irrigation and residue composting, become income through verified sustainability programmes.

// For the judges

Why this one wins

Innovation

It connects the whole season, from soil to sale, instead of solving one step. The what-if planner shows the trade-off before money is spent.

Impact

It's built for the 86% of farmers that enterprise tools ignore, in their language, with the core app free forever.

Feasibility

It isn't a mockup. The planner, AI advisor, photo diagnosis, network and admin console all work today at the app link.

Scalability

One agronomist serves thousands through the console, and every farmer who joins makes the network more useful to the next.

Viability

Four revenue lines, all paid by businesses that already spend money to reach farmers, so farmers never carry the cost.

Risks we've planned for

RiskHow we handle it
Farmers don't trust app adviceLaunch through FPOs and KVKs farmers already trust. Every recommendation shows its reasoning, and the network lets farmers verify it with each other.
Wrong advice causes crop lossThe AI never gives chemical doses, flags uncertainty, and points to local experts. An agronomist reviews the crop data behind every recommendation.
Poor connectivity and basic phonesA light, fast app first, with WhatsApp and voice next so the advice doesn't depend on a smartphone.
Farm data misuseFarmers own their data. It's shared with lenders or insurers only with explicit consent, and aggregate insights are anonymised.
